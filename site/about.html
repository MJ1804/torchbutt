What Is TorchButt?

🔥

TorchButt is a meme. A butt with a torch. Emerald flame on top. That's it. That's the joke.

Inspired by the legendary Dick Butt meme tradition (K.C. Green, 2006), TorchButt follows in the footsteps of internet absurdity — a single character, a single concept, deployed across every meme format imaginable.

Why?

Why not? Memes don't need permission. TorchButt exists because someone looked at Dick Butt and thought: "what if instead of a... you know... it was a torch?" And then someone made it real.

The emerald flame comes courtesy of LibertyTorch — the decentralized raffle protocol with a torch obsession. TorchButt is the mascot. The flame is the brand. The butt is the meme.

The Format

TorchButt works like any great meme:

🎭 The Reveal — Setup panels, then TorchButt in the final frame
🎭 The Reaction — TorchButt as a caption reaction image
🎭 The Crossover — TorchButt inserted into any scene, anywhere
🎭 The Absurd Context — TorchButt in a board meeting, a textbook, a legal document

The Character

TorchButt is free to use, share, and remix. Drop the transparent PNG into your meme. Add your caption in Impact font. Publish. That's the whole system.

Just don't try to explain it to someone who's never heard of Dick Butt. Some things you have to see for yourself.
